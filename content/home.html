British Virgin Islands

Why Cruise Passengers
Choose Tortola

Tortola is the gateway to some of the Caribbean's most aspirational shore experiences — iconic granite boulders at The Baths, barefoot beach bars on Jost Van Dyke, and unhurried sailing through the Sir Francis Drake Channel. It is a port day that feels genuinely special.

Whether you crave a relaxed beach escape, a luxury catamaran with snorkel stops, or a private tour to island viewpoints and hidden coves, Tortola delivers premium Caribbean moments without rushing your return to ship.

Most excursions are built around cruise schedules, so you can enjoy a full, relaxed day ashore and still be back at the pier with time to spare.

Cruise FAQ

Tortola Shore Excursion Questions

How much time do I need in Tortola port? ›

Most cruise ships are in port 7 to 10 hours. Half day tours to The Baths or Jost Van Dyke (3 to 4 hours) and full catamaran experiences (5 to 6 hours) both work well if you confirm return times with your operator.

Can I visit The Baths on a Tortola cruise day? ›

Yes — The Baths on Virgin Gorda is one of the most popular shore excursions from Tortola. Most tours include ferry or boat transfer and allow 2 to 3 hours at the site. Book early on busy port days.

Are independent shore excursions safe for cruise passengers? ›

Licensed BVI operators run thousands of cruise tours each season. Book operators that guarantee ship return timing and read recent reviews. Verify prices and policies before you pay.

What currency is used in Tortola? ›

The British Virgin Islands use US dollars. Credit cards are widely accepted; small beach vendors may prefer cash for chairs, snacks or tips.
