Jost Van Dyke from a Tortola cruise call

Jost Van Dyke sits across the water from Tortola. A White Bay day is one of the classic BVI cruise choices, but it is a boat day, not a Road Town taxi beach. Sea state, schedules and operator logistics decide whether it fits your call.

White Bay is the beach most cruise guests mean when they ask about Jost. Beach bars and a lively waterfront atmosphere are part of the appeal when conditions and access allow. Venue opening, music and crowding change by day and season.

Do not treat ferry, private boat or organised excursion timings as fixed. Confirm meeting point, return plan and all-aboard buffer with your operator. Weather and sea conditions can shorten or cancel a water transfer.

Compare this option against The Baths from Tortola, a Tortola beach day from Road Town, or the wider Tortola tour hub.

Planning honesty

  • • Transport is operator and sea-state dependent. No guaranteed ferry, calm water or fixed journey time.
  • • Beach and venue access can change. Do not assume a named bar is open or reachable on your call day.
  • • Build a return buffer before all aboard. Schedule presence on the ship calendar does not equal excursion availability.
  • • Mobility: boat boarding and beach walking can be uneven. Ask about access before you book.
